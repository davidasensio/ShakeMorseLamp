About ShakeMorseLamp

ShakeMorseLamp is an Android application published by HandySparkSoft (D. A.). It controls the device flashlight, transmits text as Morse code through the torch, and can switch the light on in response to a shake gesture. The app runs entirely on your device and requires no account.
