# Recommended app title
ShakeMorseLamp: Flashlight

# Recommended short description
Shake for light, blink Morse, send SOS. Offline, no ads, no tracking.

# Full description
Shake your phone. The light comes on. That is the whole idea.

ShakeMorseLamp is a fast, private flashlight that does more than switch on. Double-shake to light your way without unlocking, type a message and send it in Morse code through the torch, or fire a looping SOS straight from your Quick Settings tile.

⚡ A FLASHLIGHT YOU CAN CONTROL
• Shake to turn on — double-shake to toggle the light, screen locked or not
• Adjustable sensor sensitivity, so it fires when you mean it to
• Dimmable beam on phones whose hardware supports brightness control
• Auto-off timer from 1 minute to 8 hours, so a pocketed phone never drains overnight

📡 SEND MESSAGES IN MORSE CODE
• Type any message and the torch blinks it out in real Morse code
• Loop it, or send it once
• Tune words-per-minute and the pause between repeats
• Quick signals and a history of what you have already sent

🆘 EMERGENCY SOS
• Add an SOS tile to Quick Settings and loop your emergency message with one tap
• Switch the shake gesture from "light on" to "send my emergency message"
• Write your own emergency text — it does not have to be SOS

🏠 REACH IT INSTANTLY
• Home screen widget: pin a quick phrase and transmit it with a single tap
• Quick Settings tile for SOS, no unlocking required
• Everything keeps running with the screen off

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
This app collects nothing. No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no sign-up. It does not even request the internet permission, so nothing can leave your phone — check the permission list yourself. Your settings and message history stay on your device and are deleted when you uninstall.

🌍 SEVEN LANGUAGES
English, Spanish, Catalan, German, French, Italian and Portuguese, switchable inside the app. Light and dark themes included.

Please note: ShakeMorseLamp is a signalling tool, not an emergency service, and is not a substitute for one. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number by every means available. The app produces rapid flashing light — use it with care around anyone sensitive to that, and never shine it toward drivers, aircraft or vessels.

Free. Offline. Quiet about it.
